# GATE CS 2027 syllabus authority

Checked 12 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata). The official PDFs were accessible and read. The local topic vocabulary in [syllabus.json](../blueprints/syllabus.json) operationalizes the subject areas requested by the project, checked against the PDFs; it is not a substitute for the official wording.

- [Official CS syllabus](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf)
- [Official GA syllabus](https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/GA_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf)

CS explicitly includes tabular Boolean minimization, ALU/control-unit design, memory interfacing, pipeline hazards, and CPU/I/O scheduling. Compiler data-flow topics explicitly include constant propagation, liveness and common-subexpression elimination. Networks include switching, IPv4 fragmentation/CIDR/NAT, TCP, sockets, DNS and HTTP. No inference is made here about which wording changed relative to 2026; that requires a version comparison.

GA covers verbal, quantitative, analytical and spatial aptitude. Our spatial questions must exercise transformations or patterns rather than relying only on verbal logic. Diagrams must be local or represented by unambiguous coordinates.

Topic labels such as functional dependencies and pointers are operational subtopics of the official normal-forms and C-programming areas. Each accepted question still needs a content review: membership in a topic list alone cannot prove syllabus compliance. Software frameworks, cloud certifications and machine learning are excluded.
