# 1,000 additional adapted practice questions

Twenty sets of 50 questions, each with a final answer key and worked solutions. These are lightweight adaptations of 1,000 different existing questions: the selection task is changed to invalid-answer selection or exact-pair selection. They are text-distinct variants, not 1,000 new reasoning families or wholly original problems. Original numerical data and assumptions are preserved.

The adaptation rules and answer mappings are checked programmatically. Underlying answers retain their source review limitations; this bank is not independently or human-reviewed. The original 100 mock papers are unchanged.
