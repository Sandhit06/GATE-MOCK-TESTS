## Q4

**Correct answer:** 4.25

Let t=log_2(x), so t≠0. Change of base gives log_x(16)=4/t. Thus t²=4, with t=2 or −2, giving x=4 or 1/4. Both are positive and different from 1. Their sum is 17/4=4.25. Logarithm bases below 1 are valid; discarding the negative value of t would lose a solution.

## Q8

**Correct answer:** 2.6667 (accepted range 2.66665–2.66675)

The two centres and either intersection point form an equilateral triangle. At each centre, the arc inside the other disk therefore subtends 120 degrees. Each exposed boundary arc subtends 240 degrees and has length (240/360)×2πr=4πr/3. Two such arcs give P=8πr/3, so the requested ratio is 8/3, rounding to 2.6667. The common internal arcs are not part of the union's perimeter.

## Q10

**Correct answer:** 25

A 12.5% profit on cost ₹80 requires an average selling price of ₹90. If the discounted price is s, (3/5)×100+(2/5)s=90, giving s=75. Relative to the listed ₹100, that is a 25% discount. Profit percentage is measured against cost, whereas the requested discount is measured against the listed selling price.
